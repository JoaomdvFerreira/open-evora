# D6 — Evaluation Readiness

**Status:** PLANNED
**Milestone:** `M006` — D6 Evaluation Readiness

## Objective

Determine, through a bounded canonical screen and human gate, whether any current `PRB-0001` through `PRB-0010` has a sufficiently defined, measurable, locally grounded evaluation route; explicitly retain all other Problems as conditional, hold, watch, or outside D6.

D6 is an evaluability screen. It is not evaluation execution, intervention, product, vendor, or architecture selection, and it does not automatically advance any Problem.

## Work units

### WU025 — D6 Evaluation-Readiness Screen

Screen every current Problem against these owner-approved criteria:

1. sufficiently defined evaluation unit/mechanism;
2. current local baseline;
3. measurable outcome;
4. legitimate comparator or observation route;
5. access, ethics, and privacy feasibility;
6. decision relevance; and
7. proportionality of the effort required to make the route evaluable.

For each Problem, record an evidence-grounded disposition: `EVALUATION_ROUTE_READY`, `CONDITIONAL`, `HOLD`, `WATCH`, or `OUTSIDE_D6`, along with missing conditions, non-inferences, and reconsideration triggers. `EVALUATION_ROUTE_READY` means only that a route is sufficiently specified for a later human programme gate; it does not authorize evaluation execution.

WU025 may identify, but must not create, a decision-critical research need. It must name the decision, minimum evidence, proportionate method, and stopping condition, and any later research requires normal human authorization and Gate 1.

### WU026 — D6 Human Readiness Gate & Closure

Dependent on WU025. Record the human disposition for every screened Problem, evaluate the D6 exit gate, and create a bounded handoff to a later programme phase without designing or starting that phase.

## D6 entry gate

- D5 is closed with explicit unresolved uncertainty.
- The canonical `PRB-0001` through `PRB-0010` corpus and D5 closure are the baseline.
- The owner-approved objective, readiness criteria, dispositions, and research rule govern the work.
- No external research starts without a decision-critical need and subsequent human authorization.

## D6 exit gate

- Every screened Problem has one explicit D6 disposition, missing conditions, non-inferences, and reconsideration triggers.
- Any `EVALUATION_ROUTE_READY` route is bounded by its unit/mechanism, local baseline, outcome, comparison or observation route, feasibility, and proportionality limits.
- Any proposed decision-critical research is only specified, not executed.
- Human review records all final dispositions and any bounded later-phase handoff.
- No intervention, product, vendor, architecture, evaluation execution, or automatic Problem advancement has occurred.

## Boundaries

- The D5 handoff remains binding, including conditional D4 carries for `PRB-0005` and `PRB-0009`.
- Late stakeholder evidence is normal incoming research, remains non-blocking, and requires Gate 1 before a canonical update.
- A zero-route result is valid.
