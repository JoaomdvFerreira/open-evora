## 3. Contradiction and refinement log

### `PRB-0003` — pedestrian and accessibility barriers

The APCE direct challenge established that an in-loco assessment took place and
identified concrete barrier factors. APCE additionally reported that some
barriers identified in earlier Acesso Universal versions were later subject to
intervention. This bounded partial validation and refinement does not establish
a diagnostic-to-intervention mapping by location, whether those interventions
fully corrected the barriers, which situations are resolved or pending, present
condition at each location, municipal prevalence, or lived consequence. A
current location-level inventory or a safely executable observation remains
necessary for those questions.

### `PRB-0006` — adequate and affordable housing access

The SASUE direct challenge described a structured online institutional process
with status consultation, notification and support. It therefore contradicts
or restricts a systemic institutional application/information-process
hypothesis. It does not validate housing affordability or supply, nor does it
establish student-side usability, clarity, accessibility, waiting experience or
the broader housing problem.

### Other D5 outcomes

For `PRB-0001`, `PRB-0002`, `PRB-0004`, `PRB-0005`, `PRB-0007` and
`PRB-0008`, the direct route either could not be executed proportionately or
did not resolve the named causal question. Those stopping outcomes are not
support, contradiction or validation. No contradiction was manufactured from
non-response, lack of a suitable episode, or absence of a triggered journey.
`PRB-0009` and `PRB-0010` were not expanded into D5.

  consequences; the balance of timetable, capacity and operational causes.

  rather than inconvenience or a separate operational failure.

  consequence.
- `PRB-0004`: actual everyday cycling impact, including discontinuity, safety
  and destination connectivity.

  experience, alongside the broader housing affordability and supply problem.
- `PRB-0007`: execution and effectiveness of support-navigation measures;
  whether bureaucracy/navigation materially contributes to access difficulty;

  psychological needs.

  institutional non-response does not resolve this question.
- `PRB-0009`: its existing `WATCH` questions concerning operational/service
  reliability and any narrow material information or lifecycle gap.
- `PRB-0010`: current municipality-wide road condition and its existing
  `WATCH` triggers.

## 5. `PRB-0007` route disposition

The affected-journey route is stopped as `NO_SUITABLE_CASE`: no already
available concrete support-seeking episode met the bounded criterion, and
recruitment was deliberately not expanded. The institutional DEIS route is
`PENDING_EXTERNAL_RESPONSE` and non-blocking: DEIS acknowledged the request,
asked for project context, received that context, and a substantive response is
awaited. The acknowledgement is not Evidence for or against the Problem and
does not affect its `unvalidated` status.

If no substantive response is available by 2026-09-14, the institutional route
may be recorded as `STOP_EXTERNAL_WAIT`. A later substantive response is normal
incoming research: it requires human Gate 1 review before any canonical
SRC/EVD/PRB update and does not automatically reopen or invalidate this D5
closure.
